## Scope

App-wide visual world, first surface the Team tab (`/links`). Mode: Operate. Audience: Nuzlocke/SoulLink players logging between battles on phone and PC. Every existing feature, state and accessibility rule is preserved; density at least as high as before.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every catch is a specimen with its collection label (number, locality, collectors); the run is the collection. Refuses the category default: a dark zinc dashboard of identical rounded cards with pastel type pills.

OWN-WORLD: Bottle-green shell (#0F3D30) carrying the navigation; cool sage ground (#EBEFEA), white specimen trays with hairline sage rules (#C9D6CD), ink #1A1F1C; label red #B3261E only for death and danger. Familjen Grotesk for interface text, Red Hat Mono for label data and figures. Dark theme: deep green-black ground, trays one step lighter, labels keep their double rule.

STORY: The player opens Team, sees the six trays of the current team, reads each pair's number, route, collectors and BST, evolves or marks dead in one tap, and finds Box and Gedenkstätte beside the drawer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 232px green rail left (wordmark, run box, nav, next-cap card, utility buttons); main: h1 "Team" with count line, toolbar right; drawer of 3x2 trays, side column (Box, Gedenkstätte) 268px right. Signature move: the double-ruled specimen label under every pair. Phone: rail becomes a green top bar plus the sticky tab strip.

FORM: Naturkunde-Sammlung, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK card). seed:fedbf9dc38a5d140
